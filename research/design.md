# Lattafa pitch direction

Scope: new standalone React 19/Vite/TypeScript/Tailwind v4 storefront; no existing shop or customer data is changed. UI components use src/components/ui and @ aliases. Changes reach setup, assets, typed catalog adapter, hero, carousel, variant showcase, catalog/search, product dialog, local bag, tests and deployment manifest.

Inventory: Lattafa Nigeria; prices in naira; ornate Khamrah amber decanter; pink cylindrical Yara; black-and-gold Nebras; sculptural Fakhar men and women bottles; Lattafa Pride collection; Maison Alhambra; Arabian perfume vocabulary; real source availability; supplied cloud footage; source Arabic brand mark.

Argument: Lattafa makes a signature scent approachable → a giant real perfume bottle and real price. Find scents by the feeling they evoke → a tactile curated discovery carousel. Explore Fakhar in two expressions → direct variant switch with real notes. Browse what is available in Nigeria → searchable priced collection. Learn what is inside → full product notes and source details. Make a confident selection → honest FAQ and bag handoff to the existing shop.

Direction: The object, large. Seed 487598090. Hero adapted from the dealt line-up arc to the user's explicit cinematic-video stage; the supplied carousel holds the arc interaction. Dual fields adapted to the supplied variant-switching component. User brief takes priority for rounded media stage and spring motion; no glass or gradient chrome needed. Palette from amber bottle, black brand mark, white studio product photography. One family in two weights, with a serif display accent as the second face.

Idea: On this page, Lattafa's unmistakable sculptural bottles become an interactive scent wardrobe. Live thing: choose a scent mood to filter actual products, then inspect their notes and add an available item to a persistent bag.

Copy deck: The scent of you. Arabian perfumes for every side of you. Find your signature, from the everyday to the unforgettable. Explore fragrances. A little obsession. Pick your mood. Two expressions. One signature. The collection. A world inside every bottle. Good to know. Wear a feeling.

No invented testimonials, review counts, shipping promises or discount claims. Product catalog is a dated snapshot. Checkout is a handoff to the real Lattafa store; no payment collection or simulated order submission.
